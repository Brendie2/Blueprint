Objections

Before you apply

Who is this for, and who is it not for?

It is for experienced independent B2B consultants and service providers who have delivered real work and want 1 clear service buyers can understand and choose. It is not for you if you want guaranteed clients, a done-for-you website or funnel, or ghostwriting. If you have not yet delivered this kind of work for a paying client, I will tell you honestly what to build first.

What if I do not know what to sell yet?

That is exactly why the program starts with a Reality Check and buyer/opportunity research. You do not need to arrive with a finished offer.

What if nobody wants my service?

We test commercial relevance early through buyer, problem, and competing-solution research. We do not guarantee demand, enquiries, or sales.

I do not have testimonials. Can I still join?

Yes. The Trust Signals phase helps you identify authentic evidence appropriate to your experience. We do not invent proof.

I have bought courses before and did not implement them.

This is implementation-led. You make decisions, create deliverables, and receive feedback on your actual work throughout the program.

How much time does this take?

Plan for roughly 2–4 hours per week between sessions, plus your scheduled 1-on-1 time.

What if I choose the wrong direction?

The process is diagnosis-led. As evidence emerges, we revisit earlier decisions and refine the direction.

Will I get clients?

No. The program helps you build a clear, commercially relevant service and buyer path. Sales depend on demand, buyer access, execution, and market conditions.

Why is this a pilot?

This is the first structured cohort. Founding clients receive the $347 pilot price in exchange for honest feedback that helps refine the program.
