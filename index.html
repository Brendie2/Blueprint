The gap

You may not have an expertise problem. You may have a translation problem.

People don’t meet your expertise directly. They meet your signals: your service language, profile, content, proof, offer and calls to action. From those they form a perception, and decide.

“I’m not sure what she does.”

The offer is hard to name or compare.

“I don’t know if it’s for me.”

The buyer and problem aren’t specific enough to recognise.

“I like her content, but what does she sell?”

Attention without a clear path to a decision.

The Blueprint doesn’t ask you to post more or rebrand again. It asks what the market can actually perceive.

Is it for you?

Be honest about fit.

It may fit if

  • You have real expertise and a service you are selling or shaping
  • People engage, but don’t understand, trust or choose you as much as your skills deserve
  • You want guided implementation on your actual business, not a lecture
  • You can commit weekly time and make changes in your own offer and assets

It probably doesn’t if

  • You want someone to do it all for you
  • You are looking for a guaranteed number of clients or a revenue figure
  • You don’t yet have any expertise or service to work with
  • You are not able to implement changes between sessions

Questions

Before you apply.

Is this a course?

No. It is one-to-one guided strategy and implementation on your real business, offer and market-facing assets. You do the work in your business; I provide the diagnosis, direction and review.

Will it get me clients or revenue?

I can’t promise that, and nobody honest can. The Blueprint aims to make your expertise clearer, more credible and easier to choose, then helps you read the market’s response. Results depend on your market, your offer and your implementation.

Do I have to apply first?

Yes. I review every application for fit before any payment, so the engagement is right for where you are. Expect a reply within 2–3 business days.

Do I need to do the Clarity Call or the Scorecard first?

The free Scorecard is a good starting point, and the $27 Clarity Call can help if you are unsure which issue matters most. Neither is a requirement; the application is how we decide fit.

How much time will it take each week?

It depends on your capacity, and the application asks what you can realistically commit. The work includes a session each week plus implementation between sessions.

What are the payment and cancellation terms?

The investment is $400, paid through Selar after your application is approved. The engagement terms, including rescheduling and cancellation, are shared with you in writing before you pay.
